    What this page can and cannot prove
    
    
      
  • The browser simulates Kitty: bold does not brighten, dim text is blended toward the background by dim_opacity, and inverse swaps default colors. Font rendering and gamma differ from Kitty; confirm the final palette in Kitty itself.
    • 
      
  • The “after” 256-color grays are an approximation of palette_generate semantic (Kitty 0.47+), interpolating the grayscale ramp from background to foreground. Kitty's exact values may differ slightly.
    • 
      
  • Trade-offs are shown in the scorecard: the parchment foreground sits closer to bright cyan (dark); a paper-colored color0 makes literal black text vanish, and white-on-red badges stay unreadable so that white text stays readable (light). On the khaki, “yellow” can only be a brown-orange ink.
    • 
      
  • Not fixable by the palette: white-on-red/blue ls badges in the dark theme, and any app drawing its own RGB colors.
